Cadence 1.4

I fixed a lot of stuff
that was bothering me.

I tried to use Cadence more like an actual user and kept finding parts that felt awkward, cramped or harder to find than they should be. So I fixed a lot of that. Rhyme Lab got a huge upgrade too, Notes has a real workspace now, and image export is much less likely to give you something weird.
