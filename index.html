Getting started

Open the example song, or start a new one. Cadence saves as you write. You can replay the tour in Settings.

Open Overview to compare line lengths or find a word you keep repeating. Choose a result to jump to that lyric. Turn on Syllables to see a syllable count for each word on your current line. Cadence shows suggested breaks where it knows them.

To practice a verse, open the song's BPM control. Tap along to set the tempo, then start the metronome. The volume control adjusts the click.

You can write without an account and set up online backup later.

Where is my writing saved?

Cadence saves your writing and its history on this device. To take your text to another app, export it as Markdown. On desktop, your Markdown/Git folder also holds a readable copy of your vault.

Google Drive syncs your writing. Each device keeps its own version history. Before uninstalling on Android, export a complete backup so you can recover both.

How do I connect Google Drive?
  1. Open Settings, then Sync.
  2. Choose Google Drive, then Continue with Google. Use the same Google account on each device.
  3. If Drive already has a vault, review the changes Cadence proposes before you apply them.
  4. Choose how often to sync. To see what changed on another device, choose Download changes.

Update every connected device to 1.8 or later before syncing saved themes and note tags. GitHub backup works on desktop only. Drive needs an internet connection. You can still write and look up rhymes offline.

What if I edit the same song on both devices?

Read both versions, then choose which one to keep. You can keep both when that option appears. Cadence saves a recovery copy before it applies your choice.

If you edit while the review is open, Cadence may ask you to compare the newer copies before you continue.

How do I install or update on Android?

Download the signed APK below and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install it.

Install updates over the app you already have. Do not uninstall first. If Android refuses to update an old test build, it may have a different signing key. Before replacing it, export a complete backup and check your writing on another device. Uninstalling can erase your local writing and history. Drive sync leaves history on the device.

Cadence is not on Google Play yet.

Why might Windows show an unknown-publisher warning?

I don't have a Windows Authenticode certificate for Cadence yet. Windows can show that warning even when the updater signature and download checksum are valid.

Download from the official release below. If the file came from somewhere else or its checksum differs from the published one, stop before installing.
